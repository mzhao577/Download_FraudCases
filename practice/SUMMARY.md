## Sources searched

### DOJ - U.S. Department of Justice (Main Justice + all 93 U.S. Attorney's Offices)

Primary publisher of federal health care fraud criminal charges, convictions, sentencings, False Claims Act civil settlements and the annual National Health Care Fraud Takedowns. Covers Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE and private/commercial insurers.

- https://www.justice.gov/news
- https://www.justice.gov/criminal/criminal-fraud/health-care-fraud-unit
- https://www.justice.gov/civil/false-claims-act
- https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr

### HHS-OIG - U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office of Inspector General

Publishes the government-wide enforcement-action database (criminal, civil and Civil Monetary Penalty actions, including state Medicaid Fraud Control Unit cases), audits and evaluations of Medicare/Medicaid improper payments, and the LEIE exclusions list.

- https://oig.hhs.gov/fraud/enforcement/
- https://oig.hhs.gov/reports/all/
- https://oig.hhs.gov/exclusions/
- https://oig.hhs.gov/fraud/medicaid-fraud-control-units-mfcu/

### FBI - Federal Bureau of Investigation (national + 55 field offices)

Lead investigative agency for federal health care fraud; field-office press releases cover cases against Medicare, Medicaid and private/commercial insurers.

- https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases
- https://www.fbi.gov/investigate/white-collar-crime/healthcare-fraud
- https://www.fbi.gov/contact-us/field-offices

### CMS - Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services

Payer-side program-integrity announcements, Medicare/Medicaid improper-payment and fraud-prevention reporting, provider-enrollment moratoria and Strike Force partnership news.

- https://www.cms.gov/about-cms/contact/newsroom
- https://www.cms.gov/data-research/monitoring-programs/medicare-fee-service-compliance-programs
- https://www.cms.gov/data-research/monitoring-programs/improper-payment-measurement-programs

### GAO - U.S. Government Accountability Office

Independent audits and testimony on Medicare/Medicaid fraud risk, improper payments and program-integrity oversight - useful context documents for the case corpus.

- https://www.gao.gov/reports-testimonies
- https://www.gao.gov/health-care

### Considered but not scraped
